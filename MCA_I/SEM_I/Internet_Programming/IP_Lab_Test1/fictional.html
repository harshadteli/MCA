The about Aura

The story follows humanity’s first major attempt at interstellar colonization. A giant generation ship, designed as a massive Stanford torus (a ring-shaped space habitat), leaves Earth in the year 2545 CE. It is heading toward the Tau Ceti star system, which is 11.9 light-years away, traveling at a fraction of the speed of light.

The narrative begins nearly 160 years into the voyage as the ship is finally decelerating toward its destination. Over multiple generations, the ship's closed-loop ecological biomes (which mimic various Earth environments) have begun to break down, face severe resource depletion, and experience technical glitches. The crew must navigate these compounding crises while preparing to land on a habitable, uncharted moon they name Aurora.
